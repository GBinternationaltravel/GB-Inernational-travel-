Gilgit Airport (GIL) and Skardu Airport (KDU) are the commercial airports we use for Gilgit-Baltistan flight requests. Hunza does not have its own commercial airport.

A fare on this website is a price we have published. It is not a live airline seat and it is not a ticket. Our team confirms availability and the price before an e-ticket is issued.
